# Stage 1 workspace and companion
Mode: Operate. Scope: macOS Electron desktop at 1000–1440px; a 200px companion and its compact expanded panel.

## Thesis
A calm desk for lecture materials, with a living glass companion beside it. Students can get from PDF to an editable, source-linked topic without configuring anything.

## Audience and task
University students organize courses, open slides, and resume a topic. Actual imported materials, editable notes and local activity are the proof. No fabricated learning metrics.

## Direction
The user pins liquid glass, iris/lavender/icy blue, readable system typography, and SVG Yoyo. Code-first implementation follows their explicit autonomous functional-build instruction; no mockup approval is requested or stored as a standing workflow preference. Seed 299283e5 assigned grounded candidate 6: the study desk with a quiet reading rail. Other considered structures: library shelves, journal index, course timetable, subject atlas, research binder, study desk, slide sorter. The brief controls visual material.

## First viewport
A narrow translucent course rail. A warm compact welcome and an immediately actionable create-course control. A broad pale glass study surface pairs concise next-step guidance with original hovering Yoyo. Real course cards below; recent activity is a low-noise list. Empty state offers a clearly labelled optional sample PDF. No fake metrics.

## Signature interaction
Yoyo breathes and floats gently. Opening it unfolds a compact frosted panel with explicit preview states and local active-topic context. It remains a separate always-on-top window above other apps.

## Constraints and risk
Keyboard focus, reduced motion, strong text contrast, light/dark themes. Avoid overwhelming glass. No live voice, screen access, AI generation or mastery claims. Native window dragging and multi-display recovery need runtime verification. Topic roadmaps are heuristic, editable and source-linked.
