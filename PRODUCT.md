# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

Electron desktop application, targeting macOS first. React is rendered inside isolated desktop windows.

## Stack
User-specified Electron, React, TypeScript; token-based CSS; lightweight SVG mascot.

## Users
University students studying lecture PDFs, notes, websites, and problem sets alongside other applications.

## Product Purpose
One connected learning workspace and desktop companion. Stage 1 established local course organization, PDF reading, editable roadmaps and a floating mascot. The user approved that design and authorized Stage 2 voice, permission-based screen awareness, annotations and connected local memory.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Stage 2 is authorized. Real ElevenLabs conversations and OpenAI visual analysis require user-configured credentials. Local study data persists on this Mac; selected topic context, audio and explicitly shared images go to the configured providers. Never observe private windows, retain screenshots in the local database, or continue capture after pause/stop. No fal integration, generated lesson engines, computer control or simulated learning analytics. Stage 3 requires explicit approval. Preserve the canonical CoderEren/yoteach repository; use a separate branch; never merge without approval.

## Brand Commitments
Name: yoteach. Tagline: A tutor that sees what you see. Mascot: replaceable Yoyo, a hovering pear-shaped frosted lavender and icy-blue creature. Premium restrained liquid glass, readable surfaces, system typography, iris accents. Intelligent, approachable and concise tone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard navigation, clear focus, reduced motion, readable contrast, light and dark themes. Explain empty, loading and error states. Never equate time spent with understanding.

## Evidence on Hand
The user supplied a complete product specification. The Stage 1 implementation and design were approved. The user supplied a screenshot of an oversized black companion surface after hide/show; this is a regression target. No personal lecture documents were supplied. Sample materials must be explicitly labelled and optional.

## Product Principles
- Desktop companion and workspace share persistent local context.
- Student control over data and permissions.
- Honest previews and explicit stage boundaries.
- Useful organization before AI integration.
